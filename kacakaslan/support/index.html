FAQ

The game won't open or freezes during a run

Send us your iPhone or iPad model, iOS version and the screen where it happened. You can attach a screenshot.

A purchase is missing

Use Shop → Restore purchases in the game. Characters, the Starter Pack and Remove Interstitial Ads are restored; coin and Gold Paw packs are consumable and can't be restored. If the issue continues, include the product name and approximate purchase time. Keep passwords and card details private.

Where is my progress saved?

Only on this device. There are no accounts; deleting the game deletes your progress.

I want my data deleted

See the privacy policy. You can send your request to sekoylmz14@gmail.com.
